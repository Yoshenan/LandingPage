⚡ Quick Guide: How to Use

3 Core Modules
1

Forms & Admin

  • Fill & Submit: Complete form details, minimize/maximize panels as needed, and submit to trigger approval.
  • Verify Forms: Admins can review pending requests to accept or reject them. Accepted entries are saved.
  • Manage Users: Admins register, assign company credentials, and update user permissions.
2

Audit & Features

  • Activity Audits: User activities are logged and organized automatically by assigned company.
  • Dark Mode: Toggle theme seamlessly using the sun/moon icon.
  • Flexibility: Sidebars and forms are collapsible. Forms can also be submitted directly via Telegram.
